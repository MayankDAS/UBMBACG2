Mission

We equip Western New York organizations with practical, actionable strategy while building the next generation of business leaders.

UB MBA Consulting Group is a student-led consulting organization at the University at Buffalo School of Management. Our MBA and MS consultants partner with Western New York businesses and nonprofits to deliver practical, data-driven recommendations at no cost. Clients gain rigorous analysis and an outside perspective; our members gain real client experience and leadership skills that set them apart.

Purpose
Help Western New York organizations solve business challenges, identify opportunities for growth, and adapt to a changing business environment through strategic analysis, data-driven insights, and practical technology solutions.
Who we serve
Western New York businesses, startups, nonprofits, and organizations.
